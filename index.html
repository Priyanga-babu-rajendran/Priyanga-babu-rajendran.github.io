Research & projects

Ongoing
Research

Adaptive Personalized Hybrid Quantum-Classical Federated Learning for Privacy-Preserving Intrusion Detection

  • Developing a personalized hybrid quantum-classical federated learning framework for intrusion detection, combining parameterized quantum models with classical machine learning components in a decentralized training setting.
  • Integrating Quantum Neural Networks (QNNs) with Differential Privacy (DP) mechanisms to enable privacy-preserving model training while limiting exposure of sensitive client data.
  • Designing an adaptive client-aggregation strategy to account for client-level heterogeneity and improve personalization, robustness, and collaborative model performance across distributed participants.
  • Investigating the effectiveness of the proposed framework for network intrusion detection using NSL-KDD, UNSW-NB15, and CIC-IDS2017 datasets.
  • Conducting comparative evaluation using classification performance, privacy, robustness, and federated-learning related metrics to assess the proposed hybrid architecture against conventional approaches.
Applied
Project

SignTalk — Continuous Word-Level Indian Sign Language Recognition

  • Developed a hybrid AI-based sign language recognition system for real-time, word-level Indian Sign Language gesture recognition and gesture-to-text translation.
  • Integrated YOLOv5-based object detection with MediaPipe landmark extraction and an SVM-based classification pipeline to identify and classify sign language gestures from video input.
  • Incorporated an NLP-based grammar correction component to transform sequentially recognized signs into more coherent textual output.
  • Evaluated the system on a custom dataset containing 82 Indian Sign Language words, achieving approximately 97% recognition accuracy.

Research

X-ray Diffraction Analysis and Cell Parameter Calculation of Reported Silicates

  • Performed X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis of reported silicate materials to investigate their crystallographic characteristics and structural properties.
  • Applied Bragg's law and standard crystallographic relations to analyse diffraction peaks and determine corresponding interplanar spacings and unit-cell parameters.
  • Calculated lattice parameters from experimental diffraction data and examined the resulting crystallographic values against reported reference data.
  • Interpreted diffraction patterns and evaluated the agreement between calculated structural parameters and values reported in the literature.
